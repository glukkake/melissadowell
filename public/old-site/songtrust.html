Songtrust

Songtrust is a web app that helps musicians and music publishing companies collect their royalties for songs played worldwide. I worked as the Senior Product Designer on the product team with a small team of engineers. Our main focus was adding new features from stakeholders and migrating the codebase to React. I created a design system based on Material UI that matched the 2021 redesign for the Songtrust brand.
