## Context

Narwhal Skewers are cute, functional stainless-steel barbecue skewers shaped like narwhals. The project brought together a number of designers and contractors to establish both the product and the brand in time for a Kickstarter campaign — incorporating a business, building a presence through press and social media, producing merchandise to incentivize sales, and designing two rounds of packaging and a full suite of business identity. The Kickstarter launched in 2013 and raised $17,178 from 304 backers — more than double its $8,500 goal; 2014 brought the first production round and product launch. Narwhal BBQ Skewers are sold through multiple retailers worldwide.

## Process

Narwhal Skewers began with sketches and clay sculpts to find the size and shape. A key detail was the tactile feel — the tail loops around your finger for a good grip. With help from CAD and industrial designers to 3D-scan, shape, and create the joiner between skewer and handle, we tested variations in 3D-printed sandstone and by hand, resolving issues like skewer size and carving out mass for a lighter handle while refining the perfect narwhal shape.

Once the design was set, we made the final prototype in 3D-printed stainless steel and used it to build the campaign. We commissioned a short video that walked through the build and featured a barbecue with the skewers as proof of concept, and designed custom merchandise for backer rewards — die-cut stickers with postcard backs made to ship through the mail, hand-printed t-shirts and aprons featuring the Meat Eater, Vegetarian, and later-unlocked Sweet Tooth S'mores narwhals, foil-stamped storage bags, and custom printed tubes. Several artists contributed posters and original artwork as additional rewards.

Once funded, the skewer went to a factory in China for a production sample: the best materials were selected, the design refined for molding, and the artifacts of 3D printing smoothed out in the move to lost-wax casting.
